# Self-hosted fonts

The site bundles the Latin weight-400 WOFF2 files from Fontsource packages, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Instrument Serif uses its real italic WOFF2 file; DM Sans uses its normal style. The copied license texts are in this directory.

- Instrument Serif upstream: https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif
- DM Sans upstream: https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-fonts
- Fontsource Instrument Serif package: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/instrument-serif
- Fontsource DM Sans package: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/dm-sans
